## Internet and GPS for your car, anywhere

ESPNetLink is the LTE and GPS dongle for WiCAN PRO. Plug it into the WiCAN PRO's USB port and the WiCAN has internet over the mobile network wherever the car is, plus a GPS position for Home Assistant, the logger and your rules. On a computer it is a USB modem with its own web interface, a WiFi access point and a serial console.

[Start with WiCAN PRO](getting-started/with-wican-pro.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
</div>
</div>

<dl class="facts" markdown>
<dt>Mobile network</dt><dd>LTE Cat M1 and NB-IoT (Quectel BG95-M5), one nano SIM, your carrier's plan</dd>
<dt>Position</dt><dd>GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou and QZSS (Quectel LC76G) with assisted GPS, an antenna inside the case</dd>
<dt>Connects to</dt><dd>USB (a network adapter and a serial console), its own WiFi access point, a WiFi network</dd>
<dt>Setup</dt><dd>Nothing to type with a WiCAN PRO. From a computer: a web page at `http://192.168.7.1`</dd>
<dt>Firmware</dt><dd>Updated by the WiCAN PRO it is plugged into, or from its own web page</dd>
</dl>
